Rooms & prices

Ten rooms with hot water you can count on

Six standard twins, two deluxe doubles with the mountain view, one family room and a six-bed trekkers dorm. Every bed has a blanket and a power point; every room has an attached bathroom and a hot shower.

Prices are per room, per night, in Nepali Rupees. Cash on arrival - no prepayment, no booking fee.

Good to know

House rules and policies

  • Check-in from 12:00 noon. Arriving late from the pass? Call ahead and we hold your room.
  • Check-out by 10:00. Free luggage storage after check-out.
  • Payment cash in NPR at the lodge, NPR or USD notes accepted. No prepayment.
  • Cancellation free up to 24 hours before arrival. Trekking emergency? Message us on WhatsApp.
  • Children under 6 stay free with parents. Extra mattress NPR 500.
  • Meals breakfast, packed lunch and dinner served daily 06:00 - 21:00.
