Getting here

From Kathmandu to Chame

  • Kathmandu → Besisahar
    Tourist bus or local bus, six to seven hours on the Prithvi Highway.
  • Besisahar → Chame
    Shared jeep, about eight bumpy hours on the mountain road. Leave early - the road closes after dark.
  • On foot
    Trekking in, it is two to three days from Besisahar via Bhulbhule, Jagat and Danaque.

Tell us your arrival day and we will keep a room and a hot dinner waiting, even if the jeep is late.
