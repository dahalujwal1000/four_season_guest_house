Sleeping at 2,710 m is part of the training

Chame is where the Annapurna Circuit stops being a walk and starts being a mountain trek. Here is what we tell our guests every evening over dinner.

Route

Where you are on the map

  • Besisahar → Chame
    Two to three walking days, or one long jeep ride on the rough road. Most trekkers arrive tired and dusty.
  • Chame (2,710 m) - rest day
    A short walk up to the hot springs beyond the village, or a gentle climb for the view back down the valley. This is the cheapest altitude training you will ever get.
  • Chame → Upper Pisang (3,300 m)
    Five to six hours past Telekhu and Bhratang, through pine forest with Paungda Danda - the huge rock wall - ahead of you.
  • Pisang → Manang (3,540 m)
    Six to seven hours via Ngawal or the lower road through Humde. Another acclimatisation day in Manang before the high pass.
  • Manang → Thorong La (5,416 m)
    Five to six days further with the required rest days. Start before sunrise on pass day.

Altitude

Acclimatisation and AMS

Altitude sickness is the one thing that ends treks early. It is not about fitness - it is about climbing slowly.

Warning signs

  • Headache that does not fade with paracetamol
  • Nausea, vomiting or loss of appetite
  • Dizziness, poor balance, feeling drunk
  • Breathlessness at rest, or waking up gasping
  • Confusion or drowsiness - descend immediately

What works

  • Above 3,000 m sleep no more than 300-500 m higher each night
  • Climb high, sleep low - a day walk then back down to Chame
  • Drink 3-4 litres a day, refill boiled water at the lodge
  • Skip alcohol and heavy dinners on arrival day
  • If symptoms get worse, go down. Diamox is not a fix.
Packing

What to carry, what to leave

  • Layers: thermal base, fleece, windproof shell, down jacket above 3,500 m
  • Sleeping bag rated to -10 C for lodges above Manang
  • Head torch, spare batteries, power bank (charging costs money up high)
  • Water bottle and purification tablets - we refill boiled water, no plastic needed
  • Sun cream, lip balm, sunglasses: the UV at altitude is brutal
  • Cash in NPR - there is no ATM past Besisahar and cards are useless
Season

When to trek

October and November bring the clearest skies and the busiest lodges. March and April are warmer with rhododendrons in bloom. December to February is cold but quiet and beautiful. Avoid the monsoon months of June to early September - the road washes out and the views vanish.

Even in peak season we keep a few rooms for walk-ins, but trekkers who booked ahead always sleep better.
